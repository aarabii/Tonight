Prisoners is a 2013 American thriller film directed by Denis Villeneuve.

Synopsis[edit]

In Pennsylvania, carpenter Keller Dover (Hugh Jackman) and his family attend Thanksgiving dinner at the home of their friends, Franklin (Terrence Howard) and Nancy Birch[2]. After dinner, the families' two young daughters walk back to the Dovers' house and vanish near a dilapidated RV parked on the street.

Detective Loki (Jake Gyllenhaal) arrests the RV driver, Alex Jones (Paul Dano), but police release him due to lack of physical evidence[citation needed]. Convinced that Alex knows where the girls are, a desperate Keller abducts Alex and locks him inside an abandoned apartment building to extract a confession before time runs out, while Loki races to follow a labyrinth of clues across the town.
